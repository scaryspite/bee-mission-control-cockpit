Bee Mission Control

Bee Mission Control is a personal productivity and organization application that brings schedules, tasks, reminders, files, and connected services into one workspace.

It helps its user review upcoming commitments, locate relevant information, organize day-to-day work, and coordinate personal workflows from a single interface.

Connected Google services

With user authorization, Bee Mission Control can use Google Calendar information to organize schedules and Gmail information to locate relevant work information requested by the user.

Google account access occurs only after authorization through Google OAuth and is used to provide user-requested features.
